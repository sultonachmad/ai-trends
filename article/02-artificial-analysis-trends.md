# AI Trends: Benchmarks, Costs, and Model Analysis

**Source:** [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/trends)
**Focus:** Quantitative tracking of AI model performance, costs, speed, and architecture trends

---

## Sections Covered

### 1. AI Progress
- Tracks frontier language model intelligence over time using the Intelligence Index (v4.3.2).
- Tracks capital expenditure (capex) by major tech companies (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon, Oracle) in billions of USD per quarter.
- Compares leading models from each AI lab (Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, and others).

### 2. Efficiency
- Tracks cost per Intelligence Index task by threshold (>=10, >=20, >=30, >=40, >=50, >=60) over time.
- Tracks output speed in tokens per second across the same thresholds.
- Key trend: costs falling while performance rises.

### 3. Country Analysis
- Compares frontier intelligence by country: United States, China, UAE, and France.
- Includes open-weights view and leading-models-by-country view.
- US and China remain the two main AI hubs.

### 4. Open Source Models
- Compares open-weights vs. proprietary intelligence.
- License categories: Open Weights, Commercial Use Restricted, Non-commercial.
- Gap between open and proprietary is narrowing.

### 5. Model Architecture
- Compares Mixture of Experts (MoE) vs. dense models.
- MoE popularity is rising: reduces computation per input while scaling to more parameters.
- Tracks total vs. active parameters (billions) and median context length by quarter.

### 6. Training Analysis
- Tracks training tokens (trillions) by model.
- Relationship between training data volume and intelligence measured.

## Key Observations

- **Capex as commitment indicator:** Large compute investments signal continued AI development push.
- **MoE architecture trend:** Rising adoption for efficiency at scale.
- **Open-source momentum:** Open-weight models closing the gap with proprietary ones.
- **Cost reduction:** Inference costs decreasing over time across all performance tiers.
- **Speed improvement:** Output tokens per second increasing consistently.
